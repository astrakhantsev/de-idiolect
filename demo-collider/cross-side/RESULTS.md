# Component 3 — cross-side definition-mediated matching (the §5.5 shape)

## Headline (finding first)

Two cells, run as the entry's define-then-verify operation on the collider corpus, each emitting the three artifacts (typed SKOS relation · shared core · per-side residues). The cells were selected to have **known-opposite** answer keys, and **on both, the verify step produced the answer-keyed result:**

- **Cell 1 (accretion mechanism × white-dwarf survival bound) → dependent (answer key), and found.** Both draws (sonnet + opus) identify the shared premise as the **accretion mechanism** (growth rate set by a capture radius and a mass flux, dM/dt = π r_c² F) and state that if it were wrong, both would fail — recovering, blind, the dependence Giddings & Mangano *actually built* (the bound is the mechanism evaluated at stellar density; the §4.1 sentence that forward-references white dwarfs was withheld from the excerpt). Beyond the answer key, both typed the relation `broadMatch` (a model output, below).
- **Cell 2 (Hawking-evaporation/decay × cosmic-ray survival) → not a shared premise (answer key), and none found.** Both draws return **NONE** — "a topical resemblance, not a shared load-bearing premise" (sonnet); "falsifying one leaves the other untouched" (opus).

**What two author-selected cells do and do not establish.** They establish that, on these two specific pairs, the verify step returned the expected positive and the expected negative. They do **not** establish an operation-level "discrimination" capability — two hand-picked cells cannot. Cell 2 is the *designed control*: its purpose is to show that cell 1's positive is not an artifact of the step firing on any pair that is "both about black-hole safety." That is what it shows, for this pair.

**This is a two-cell, author-selected, demonstration-grade validation** on a closed case with an answer key. It is not a recall or retrieval measurement, adds nothing to §5, and changes no at-submission grade. Same-family (sonnet + opus) draws are a correlated read; convergence is weak evidence and divergence informative (see the cell-2 relation split below).

## Protocol

Two live isolated stages (fresh cwd + credentials-only HOME + pinned `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` + all tools disallowed — so the caller's global config, which carries this project's "vocabulary seam / dependence" framing, cannot leak the answer). Code: [`run_cell.sh`](run_cell.sh). Excerpts, prompts (with hashes), per-draw isolation manifests, and raw outputs are under [`runs/`](runs/); frozen usage excerpts under [`excerpts/`](excerpts/).

1. **Define** (per concept, sonnet): a community-neutral constrained definition from that concept's frozen usage excerpt only — ordinary words, **no proper nouns** (no names of people, methods, fields, instruments, or specific objects). This is ENTRY §4.1 stage 2.
2. **Verify** (per cell, sonnet + opus): given the two concepts' **raw usage excerpts** — blind, labelled only "Passage 1/2", not told they share a case or which is theory vs bounds — decide whether they share a *load-bearing* core premise, and emit the three artifacts. Usage-based, **not** definition-against-definition (§5.5: two definitions through one wordlist resemble by construction, a false-positive risk). This is ENTRY §4.1 stage 3.

The provenance header on each excerpt (which names the paper, section, and side) is stripped before any draw sees it.

**Provenance of the committed run.** The receipts under `runs/` were generated by the pre-review `run_cell.sh`. An adversarial code review then hardened the script — failed-draw exit propagation, pinning the temp base to `/tmp`, an expanded tool-denylist, plus `curl --fail` and slice anchor-checks in the corpus scripts — changes to failure-handling and defensiveness, **not** to the prompts or the isolation actually in effect. The committed run's blindness is verifiable independently of that fix: its draws' temp cwd was created under `/tmp` (which has no `CLAUDE.md` ancestor on this system) and the fresh HOME carried only the credentials file, so no global or project `CLAUDE.md`/memory could reach a draw. Prompt and output `sha256` in the manifests remain valid.

## Cell 1 — accretion-growth mechanism (theory) × white-dwarf survival bound (bounds)

Excerpts: [`excerpts/A1-accretion-mechanism.txt`](excerpts/A1-accretion-mechanism.txt) (Giddings & Mangano §4.1), [`excerpts/B1-wd-survival-bound.txt`](excerpts/B1-wd-survival-bound.txt) (§7.4).

| Artifact | sonnet | opus |
|---|---|---|
| **Typed relation** | `broadMatch` (P1 broader, P2 narrower) | `broadMatch` (P2 the narrower) |
| **Shared core** | the accretion rate law dM/dt ∝ r_c²·F (capture radius × mass flux); "if that premise were wrong… Passage 1's framework would be undermined too" | "a small [object] embedded in dense matter grows by gravitational capture/accretion… governed by a capture radius and mass flux"; "both would fail" |
| **Per-side residue** | P1: general formalism (capture vs Schwarzschild radius, flux sources, worst-case methodology). P2: the production scenario, magnetic-field constraints, liquid-vs-solid argument, and the survival-time inference | P1: the general formalism, applicable to any host body. P2: the production context, the *stopping* requirement, white-dwarf-specific factors, and "**most distinctively — the empirical safety-argument logic** … a form of reasoning entirely absent from Passage 1" |

**Answer key (the case is closed):** dependent. Giddings & Mangano derive the white-dwarf bound *from* the accretion model; §4.1 closes with "we will also perform similar calculations for accretion of white dwarfs and of neutron stars" (withheld from the verify excerpt to keep the judgment blind). **What is independently keyed here is only the dependent/not-dependent fact, and both draws returned "dependent."** The relation label, its direction, and the per-side residues are **model outputs, not independently keyed** — reported as the operation's three artifacts, not as validated classifications. As a directed triple the label reads `P2 skos:broadMatch P1` (P1, the general mechanism, is the broader concept), following the entry's convention that the subject is the narrower side (§4.1). Same-family (sonnet + opus) agreement on these labels is a correlated read, not corroboration.

**The most interesting *illustrative* residue is opus's** (a single model output, not keyed). It described not only the shared accretion core but a *separable* premise the bound layers on top: the empirical "a comparable body survived, so we survive" inference. This mirrors the effective-independence anatomy the entry describes — one reassurance is *partly* a re-expression of the mechanism (dependent) and *partly* a genuinely separate empirical step, so a reader who counts "the theoretical accretion analysis" and "white dwarfs are old, so we're safe" as two independent reassurances would be double-counting the shared accretion core. Offered as an illustration of what the residue artifact can surface, not as a validated finding.

Excerpts: [`excerpts/A2-hawking-decay.txt`](excerpts/A2-hawking-decay.txt) (§2.1), [`excerpts/B2-cosmic-ray-survival.txt`](excerpts/B2-cosmic-ray-survival.txt) (§2.2).

**Answer key:** complementary, not shared-premise. These are *alternative* legs of the safety case (black holes decay via Hawking radiation — *or*, if they were stable, cosmic-ray-produced ones would have been stopped and observed). Falsifying one does not touch the other. **Both draws returned NONE, matching the answer key.** The relation label diverges across the two same-family tiers (`NO-MATCH` vs `relatedMatch`) — the informative kind of divergence: both agree there is no shared core; they disagree only on whether "both are high-energy robustness arguments" earns a `relatedMatch` (opus, more precise) or a `NO-MATCH` (sonnet, stricter). Neither invented a shared premise on this pair.

## The define stage (vocabulary-stripping, shown)

The neutral definitions contain **none of the eight listed target proper nouns** — a grep of the A1/B1 define outputs for `hawking|bondi|white dwarf|cosmic|black hole|LHC|giddings|neutron star` returns zero hits (a targeted check against the vocabulary that *would* have named these concepts, not a proof that no proper noun of any kind remains). The A1 definition: *"a mass-dependent parameter of a compact heavy object embedded in a surrounding medium … it determines the growth rate via dM/dt = π r_c² F, where F is the local mass flux."* The B1 definition: *"a claim … given two objects that can each capture and absorb surrounding matter, if one object's known survival to a certain age implies a lower bound on how slow its absorption process must be …"* Both describe "an object growing by absorbing its surroundings at a rate set by a capture radius and a flux" without naming a black hole, a white dwarf, or a field — the vocabulary removed, the premise still stated in neutral terms. **These definitions are the stage-2 artifact only; verification (above) runs on the raw *usage* per §5.5, so the definitions are not the matching key in this cell** — retrieval-by-definition is §5.1's separate measurement and is not re-run here.

## Honest scope

- **n = 2 cells, one corpus, author-selected, same model family.** A demonstration of the operation and its three-artifact output, not an effect size. No retrieval/ranking was run here (that is §5.1's separate measurement); the concepts were hand-paired, so this tests the *verify/type* stage given a pair, not discovery of the pair.
- **Validation, not discovery.** The case is closed, so cell 1's "correct" answer is a dependence physicists already established. Recovering a known dependence blind is checkable and is the point (a false positive would have been a fabricated dependence; a false negative, missing a real one). It is not evidence the operation finds dependences no expert knew.
- **The shared vocabulary is real and stated.** Giddings & Mangano use "accretion" on both the mechanism and bound sides; this is a *reconciled* seam, consistent with §5.2 (established vocabulary is memorized) and §7 (the tool's value concentrates on *unreconciled* seams). What the cell adds over the term-check is the *typed, decomposed* dependence (broadMatch + the separable empirical residue), not a harder retrieval.
