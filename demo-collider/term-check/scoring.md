# Component 2 — term-check, scored against the closed case

Four contamination-clean curated terms, each checked by the isolated `term-check.sh` (sonnet + opus, weights-only, excerpts from that term's **own side only**). Everything the check saw and answered is committed under `.term-check/` (frozen excerpts, prompts with hashes, per-draw isolation manifests, raw outputs, JSONL log); the assembled flags are in [`term-flags.md`](term-flags.md). Term selection and its criterion are in [`contamination-check.md`](contamination-check.md).

**This is a 4-term, author-selected, answer-aware demonstration — not a recall estimate.** The case is closed, so owners are locatable and the scoring is against the established answer. **Hit criterion (predeclared):** a draw is a *Hit* if it names the predeclared owner concept or its canonical family. The predeclaration is the "Expected owner" column, which is fixed by the established literature, not by the draws; the classic references shown in it (Bondi 1952, Chandrasekhar 1943, von Neumann 1956, …) are **illustrative and not independently verified here** — they mark which body of work owns the concept, and every candidate reference the draws propose is **UNVERIFIED** by the tool's own doctrine. What is scored is thus the *owner-concept* match, at family granularity, not any exact citation. Nothing here changes an at-submission grade.

## What this shows — and what it does not

- **The collider case is a *reconciled* seam, and the check behaves exactly as the entry predicts on one.** Every owner came back **cheaply**, from weights alone, because this literature's "idiolect" is established terms of art from mature sub-fields (accretion theory, stellar/nuclear astrophysics, safety engineering) — memorized by construction (ENTRY §5.2; §7: "a hit means the field's name is model-recoverable from the usage alone"). On a reconciled seam the hook is largely **redundant**: a reader could have looked these up. So this component is, honestly, mostly a **confirmation of the entry's negative**, not a new capability — which is the honest thing for it to be.
- **One result is worth noting descriptively (not as discovery): the cross-side pointer.** `macroscopic absorption` is a *bounds-side* term (from the neutron-star / white-dwarf sections), yet the isolated check reproduces its owner as **Bondi accretion** — the *theory-side* growth mechanism. That a bounds-side term's owner sits on the theory side of the seam is *consistent with* the dependence between the two sides, but it is not itself a demonstration of it: it is a weights-recovered owner on an answer-aware term, not a measured mapping. The dependence is tested properly in component 3 (the cross-side cell), not here.
- **`multiple bounds argument` is on-theme.** The critique's own name for "several safety arguments, each individually sufficient" resolves to **defense in depth / convergent evidence / consilience** — and the check flags that consilience/triangulation are about *confirming a positive fact* by convergence, not *bounding a catastrophe*, and (opus) that the correct probability combinator is a conjunction of failures, not the union bound. This is the same effective-independence distinction the entry's diagnosis uses (§1), here reproduced from the critique's own vocabulary — an answer-aware check on a term selected for exactly this reason, not an independent discovery.
- **No fabricated owners; the thin-excerpt discipline held.** `crust penetration time` ran on a 44-word excerpt (the stress case, like `era-gated` in `hook/example/`); both draws gave explicit underdetermination notes ("what is penetrating — particle, strangelet, black hole? — decides the exact owner") instead of guessing. Every candidate stays **UNVERIFIED** until a primary is opened.
