- **The syndetic apparatus (see / see also cross-references)** — librarianship — Charles Ammi Cutter, *Rules for a Printed Dictionary Catalogue*, 1876 — machinery whose entire purpose is to route a searcher from the term they used to the term the collection used — **RECALLED**
