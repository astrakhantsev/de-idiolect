1. **Established name(s)**

The best match is **union/OR aggregation**: pool every distinct issue identified by any judge, then inspect or adjudicate those issues individually. Related standard names are:

- **Any-positive rule** or **parallel screening/testing** in detection theory and diagnostics.
- A **disjunctive task** in Steiner’s taxonomy of group performance: one member’s valid finding can benefit the group; it need not receive majority support.
- In review practice, **pooling findings**, **defect-list aggregation**, or **multi-evaluator inspection**. The motivation is often called the **evaluator effect**: different reviewers discover substantially different sets of problems.

If a human subsequently checks the pooled claims, the most precise description is **high-recall candidate generation followed by adjudication**. That phrase is descriptive rather than a single universally established term.
