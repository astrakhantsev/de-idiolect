# Retro on the last two weeks of eval churn

Wrapping up before the long weekend, so here's everything that moved. The synthetic ladders debate from a couple posts back finally got resolved — we're keeping the generated hard tier but relabeling it clearly instead of pretending a human wrote it, since twin runs on it this week showed only a 3-point wording gap, tighter than I expected and honestly better behaved than some of our human-authored items.

Ouroboros items are getting killed off entirely though. Went back through the billing-scenario batch and confirmed, again, that the author agent's own strengths were leaking into which items survived the filter pass. Not worth the convenience anymore.

Salt run on the full remaining suite came back clean, no score movement under randomized ids and filenames, which is one less thing to worry about heading into next quarter.

Rig diary paid off twice this cycle — once catching the reread ration cap change that broke three agents' runs, once catching a scorer config edit that would've otherwise looked like a genuine capability regression instead of a harness artifact. I'm now fully converted on logging every single config change the day it happens, even the ones that feel too small to matter, because the ones that feel too small to matter are exactly the ones I forget by Thursday.

Net result: smaller suite, better labeled, fewer surprises. Not glamorous but I'll take it going into next month.
