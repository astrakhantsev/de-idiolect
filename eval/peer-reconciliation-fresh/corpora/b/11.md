# Longitudinal Suite Health

Across six releases, the suite tracked item discrimination exhaustion rate alongside completion and calibration metrics. The item discrimination exhaustion rate doubled for basic tasks, prompting targeted replacement rather than wholesale removal. Each retained item also carried a tier floor annotation. The tier floor annotation separated expected lower-tier failures from unexpected regressions in higher-tier systems.

A subordinate authoring protocol supplied replacement tasks with executable validators. The subordinate authoring protocol was refreshed each release to limit repeated generator patterns. Matched contrast probes accompanied each replacement family. Matched contrast probes showed that distractor sensitivity declined for navigation tasks but increased for document-heavy tasks.

The strategy cardinality index was computed from successful traces after every release. The strategy cardinality index fell when agents converged on a common browser workflow, despite stable pass rates. Reflexive adjudication was applied to anonymized answers from the final two releases. Reflexive adjudication revealed persistent self-output agreement deficits after answer text was stripped.

Trajectory template carryover was measured in ordered sessions and dropped after history clearing. A resource perturbation study increased tool quotas mid-task, and the resource perturbation study showed that extra calls often amplified trajectory template carryover rather than repair it. The echo test detected inappropriate example phrasing in 4.2% of final answers. A second echo test after replacing examples reduced that rate to 0.9%.
