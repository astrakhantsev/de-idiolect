# Diversity Under Repeated Attempts

Agents completed 600 repository-repair tasks with five independent attempts per task and fresh tool sandboxes. The strategy cardinality index was calculated only from successful attempts after traces were normalized for incidental file paths. The strategy cardinality index increased from 1.3 to 2.7 between the baseline and search-augmented systems, even when their pass rates differed by less than one point.

Reflexive adjudication used anonymized final patches and rationales sampled from all systems in a round. During reflexive adjudication, the evaluated agent agreed with external graders 6.4 points less often on its own successful patches than on matched patches from peers. The effect remained after excluding outputs with identifying filenames and unusually long explanations.

Prompt templates included two worked examples with unusual connective phrases. The echo test found that agents with the highest strategy cardinality index had fewer inappropriate copied phrases than agents relying on repeated repair traces. A second echo test after paraphrasing the examples reduced copied phrasing without changing reflexive adjudication effects.
