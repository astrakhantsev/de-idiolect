DEFINITION of a concept:

⟦TERM⟧ is a check for hidden randomness in a model's output stack. You run it by submitting the exact same prompt twice within the same session and comparing the two outputs token for token. It produces a measure of how much the two runs diverge — for example, a percentage of tokens that fail to match, or a difference in where the two outputs place a citation or stop (truncate). You use it when the setting is supposedly deterministic (temperature pinned to zero) and you want to check whether a scoring or harness oddity — such as two differently-worded items getting identical scores, or an unexpected truncation — comes from real nondeterminism rather than another cause. Because the prompt and session are held constant, any difference must come from that inherent randomness. If the two runs come out exactly identical, that part of the stack is treated as behaving correctly for that item.
