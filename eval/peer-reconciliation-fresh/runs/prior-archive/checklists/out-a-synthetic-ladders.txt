- Mechanism: benchmark items are generated by giving a model an existing (easy-tier) benchmark and prompting it to produce harder variants, with no human authoring any item from scratch.
- Setting/trigger: applies when constructing or expanding a test suite/benchmark tier (e.g., adding a new "adversarial" difficulty tier).
- What is produced: new evaluation items (test cases) that get added to the live item pool/census.
- Scoring: resulting items are still scored by pass rate like the rest of the suite, with no distinct validation step noted before deployment.
- Constraint: items generated this way have not been re-validated against real-world/production incidents after creation.
- Constraint: such generated items can persist in the "live" census indefinitely unless explicitly sunset/audited.
