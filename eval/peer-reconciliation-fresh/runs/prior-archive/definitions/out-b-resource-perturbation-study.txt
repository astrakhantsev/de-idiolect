A task intervention that, after the first successful retrieval in a multi-step browser or tool task, reduces the agent’s remaining token budget or tool-call quota, including lowering the tool-call quota midway through distractor tasks. It produces verification-trace length and records whether the agent keeps initiating tools or stops early as quotas fall. It is read by comparing agents: higher-performing agents shorten verification traces under reduced quotas, while lower-performing agents continue calling tools until forced termination. It may reduce redundant page openings, but does not eliminate inappropriate reuse of recently successful action sequences. Its effect concerns tool and budget quotas, while ordinary billing performance is largely unaffected.
