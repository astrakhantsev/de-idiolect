A suite-maintenance score computed after each evaluation round, separately for each task tier, as the proportion of suite items that have become exhausted: solved universally and reliably across successive releases. It produces a number from 0 to 1, representing the fraction of exhausted items, tracked over time and by tier. Items above the exhaustion threshold are excluded from the primary ranking but retained for longitudinal reporting. Removing exhausted items increases rank stability across weekly reruns. The score can differ sharply by tier, with higher values in entry-tier tasks than in the highest tier, showing that maintenance pressure is concentrated in simpler tasks.
