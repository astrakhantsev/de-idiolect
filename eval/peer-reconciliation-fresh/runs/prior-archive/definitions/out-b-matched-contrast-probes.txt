A paired-task evaluation procedure for measuring how irrelevant added context changes an agent’s tool-use navigation. It applies to paired benchmark tasks, such as account-management or service-configuration tasks, whose versions differ only because one adds policy excerpts, tickets, decoy URLs, or appended operational context. Both versions keep the same required action or configuration change, verification or validator endpoint, and initial system state. It compares their tool-call traces and reports additional pages opened, seconds before the first valid tool call, percentage increase in exploratory tool calls, and percentage decrease in direct validation calls. It asserts the resulting change in navigation breadth under distractor context, not a change in final answer length. Traces or outputs from both variants are mixed into blinded grading pools for evaluation.
