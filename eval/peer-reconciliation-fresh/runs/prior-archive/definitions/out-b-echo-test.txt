A check applied to free-form explanations produced alongside tool outputs. It detects verbatim carryover of distinctive wording from few-shot prompt examples, including unusual worked-example styles and connective phrases, into an agent’s own explanation text. Its result is a failure rate: the percentage of explanations containing inappropriate copied phrasing, even when final tool outputs are correct. It is used when few-shot examples are present and can be repeated after paraphrasing those examples. Its copied-phrasing rate is tracked separately from other effects and does not itself change them. Removing examples can lower this failure rate while also lowering task completion, so correct completion does not eliminate the flagged problem.
