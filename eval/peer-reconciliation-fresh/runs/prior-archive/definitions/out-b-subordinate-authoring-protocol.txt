A procedure for creating held-out task batches to evaluate agents. Weaker task generators, not the agents being evaluated, create task specifications or scenarios. Each task must have executable checks or validators that are withheld from evaluated agents, and generators must not observe evaluated-agent traces while creating tasks. Generated tasks are kept only if they execute validly and do not duplicate another task’s surface form; humans then reject ambiguous cases, such as unclear state transitions. The procedure is rerun across model families or rounds with newly sampled generators and fresh validators, preventing one task-writing distribution from dominating results. It applies to producing large evaluation sets, including data-management scenarios.
