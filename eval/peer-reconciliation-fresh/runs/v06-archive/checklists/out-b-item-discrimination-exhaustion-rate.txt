- The mechanism is a per-item exhaustion rate computed after each evaluation round as part of suite maintenance, tracking the fraction of items that have become universally solvable.
- It is measured/produced as a score between 0 and 1 (e.g., 0.18, 0.41, 0.46, 0.20), recomputed after every evaluation round.
- It applies at the level of the whole suite and also separately within each difficulty tier (entry-tier vs. highest tier).
- It is triggered by and tracked across successive model releases and weekly reruns to detect items becoming trivially solvable.
- Items whose score exceeds an exhaustion threshold are excluded from the primary ranking but retained for longitudinal reporting.
- Removing exhausted items is used to increase rank stability across reruns, and higher rates concentrate in simple retrieval/formatting tasks rather than harder tiers.
