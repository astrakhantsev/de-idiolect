A blinded grading procedure that pools anonymized final patches and rationales from all systems in a round, including the evaluated agent’s successful patches and matched peer patches from the same task. External graders assess the mixed outputs, and the procedure measures the evaluated agent’s agreement with those graders on its own patches versus peer patches as a percentage-point self-output agreement gap. It applies also when task variants are mixed under distractor context. It asserts lower agreement on the agent’s own outputs, especially when rationales reuse distinctive tool-log phrasing: 7.9 points with distractor context versus 3.2 without. The deficit remains after excluding identifying filenames and unusually long explanations, after removing rationales so graders see only final actions and validators, and after paraphrasing examples to reduce copied phrasing.
