A paired-task measurement for agentic tool-use tasks. It runs a clean task and a distractor-injected version containing irrelevant policy excerpts, historical tickets, decoy URLs, or other distractor operational context. The versions keep the required action, validator or verification endpoint, and initial system state identical. It compares their tool-call traces and reports quantitative deltas: extra pages or exploratory tool calls, percentage changes in exploratory and direct validation calls, and delay to the first valid tool call. Its results assert how distractor context changes tool use, especially navigation breadth more than final answer length. Outputs from both versions are mixed into blinded grading pools.
