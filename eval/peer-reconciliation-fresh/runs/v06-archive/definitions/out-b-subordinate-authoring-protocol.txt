A procedure for producing held-out task specifications or scenarios for agent evaluation. Weaker, non-evaluated task generators create the tasks, while executable checks or validators are required and withheld from evaluated agents. The generators are prevented from seeing evaluated-agent traces. Generated tasks are filtered or reviewed for execution validity, duplicate surface form, and unambiguous state transitions; rejected tasks are excluded from the final count. The procedure is run separately for each model family or round, each time with newly sampled generators and fresh validators, so one task-writing distribution does not dominate comparisons.
