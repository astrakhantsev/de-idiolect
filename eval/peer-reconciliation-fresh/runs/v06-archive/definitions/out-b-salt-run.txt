A pre-reporting control check in agent evaluation runs. Each batch or session receives a fixed number of previously verified items with established correct judgments—eight per batch or two per session—covertly placed among regular items without positional regularity, with answers absent from judges’ briefing. Each inserted judgment is compared with its established answer. The batch or session is halted if judges reject a correct item without support or accept an incorrect result as compliant. This check occurs before aggregate metrics are retained or computed; halted batches and sessions are excluded from final results. Reporting retains only items with judgments stable across adjudicators, separately from halted cases.
