A suite-maintenance score, recomputed after every evaluation round, that gives the fraction of items that have become universally solvable. It ranges from 0 to 1 and is calculated for the full suite and separately for each difficulty tier. It tracks successive model releases and weekly reruns to identify items becoming trivially solvable. Items above the exhaustion threshold are excluded from the primary ranking but retained for longitudinal reporting. Removing these items is used to improve ranking stability across reruns. Higher values indicate that exhaustion is concentrated in simple retrieval and formatting tasks rather than in the highest difficulty tier.
