A per-task difficulty or threshold rating, assigned independently by two annotators before deployment or inspection of model results. It states the level at which a task should be expected to succeed and is a property of the task, not of a model or run. Annotators may revise it when they find hidden dependencies such as unavailable credentials; 4.7% of tasks were revised this way. Agent results are assessed against the rating: failures on tasks below an agent’s rating are flagged for audit rather than counted as ordinary misses. Analyses include only tasks with stable, agreed ratings after adjudication and exclude unresolved disagreements. Disagreement is concentrated in chained, multi-step actions rather than simple answer extraction. Ratings do not establish one monotonic capability scale: memorized procedures can pass higher-rated tasks while unfamiliar tool states defeat lower-rated ones.
