## P3 — provenance-verification yield (pre-registered on a seeded sample; amendment M2)

**Universe and extraction.** Frozen file set (`p3-provenance/input-manifest.txt`): 17 def-naming + 16 recall-backtest `out-*.md` files (+ the e2e F baseline, which contains only record IDs — 0 candidates). Two subagents extracted **539 citation occurrences** (423 def-naming, 116 backtest; 190 hedged = 35%), each with a verbatim source quote; both agents' quotes spot-verified against sources. Census note: `out-covid-R-{opus,sonnet}.md` are single-line API-error files — 2 of 16 backtest outputs contain no model output at all. Linking produced **410 unique works** (`ledger.jsonl` + `works.jsonl`; known benign under-merges disclosed in the linking script).

**Amendment M2 (logged pre-verification, size-driven):** the universe came in 3–5× the planning estimate, so the frozen three-facet protocol was applied to a seeded random sample of **100 occurrences → 92 induced works** (seed 20260719, `sample-occ-ids.json`); the unsampled remainder is not-verified, frame logged. **Extraction-recall audit** (seeded, 4 files fully re-read by me): recall ≈ **0.89–0.92**; misses skew toward secondary mentions (standards-as-codifiers, second venues, sources-list entries), which biases measured survival slightly UP if anything (`recall-audit.md`).

**Endpoints (AMENDED primary — M2 was a post-freeze, pre-verification scope amendment, so these are amended endpoints, not pre-registered ones; review fold F4).**
- **PRIMARY (amended) — occurrence survival: 66/100 = 0.66, Wilson 95% [0.563, 0.745].**
- Work-level validity among the occurrence-induced works: 60/92 = 0.65 — **exploratory** (the induced work set is popularity-biased by construction; it does not estimate validity across the 410-work universe).
- **Failure taxonomy: nonexistent 0 · biblio-major 0 · contradicted 0 · access-unverifiable 24 · claim-not-locatable-at-abstract 10** — among cases the protocol could actually check; the 24 access-unverifiable remain existence-unresolved under the strict rule.

**Review-fold sensitivities (labeled; adjudications persisted in `sensitivity_p3.py` + `p3_sensitivity.json`).** S1 eligibility: 12 sampled occurrences hand-adjudicated against the frozen owner-candidate definition, 9 INELIGIBLE (author+year-only shells like "Platt (1999)"/"Neyman & Pearson (1933)", nickname-only "PoLL"/"OLaLa", resource mentions, no-year hedges; 5 of the 9 had survived) → **61/91 = 0.67**. S2 strict-primary receipts: 15 exists=yes works rest on secondary pages (7 Wikipedia, bookseller listing, blog explainer, R-docs, textbook page, essay-about) → **50/100 = 0.50**. S3 fetch-cap compliance: 7 exists=yes works exceeded the frozen 2-fetch+Wayback stop rule (up to 6 attempts) → **58/100 = 0.58**. **S4 combined floor: 43/91 = 0.47.** Correction of an earlier claim (review F3): over-fetching CAN inflate survival — the extra attempts are precisely what produced some receipts — so the honest statement is a strictness-dependent range **0.47–0.67**, with the amended-primary 0.66 sitting at the lenient end.

**Audits per the frozen protocol.** Kill re-check set (exists=no / biblio=major / contradicted): EMPTY — there were no such verdicts to re-check. Seeded 5-work re-check of VERIFIED verdicts: **5/5 confirmed against the fetched pages** (receipt with per-work URLs + confirmations: `verified-recheck-receipt.md`; one of the five itself rests on a Wikipedia receipt and carries the S2 caveat).

**Exploratory (labeled).** (a) **Crossref re-check of the 23 access-unverifiable works, manually adjudicated per row (`crossref-adjudication.md`; review fold F6): 15 CLEAN matches + 1 variant-publication + 1 duplicate-of-a-clean-match ≈ 16–17 of 23 corroborated bibliographically** (incl. Brier 1950, Furnas 1987, Tanner & Swets 1954, Chow 1957, Star & Griesemer 1989, Lenzerini 2002); **4 spurious fuzzy hits discarded** (GLUE→"Tunable glue" Nature news; Bréal→undated data DOI; Conitzer & Sandholm→different same-author-same-year paper; Chapin book→journal item); 3 no API match, none evidence of nonexistence (workshop/pre-DOI items). Qualitatively this supports access-dominated attrition; it is NOT a precise existence rate (fuzzy matching, author field not enforced). On this run even the Wayback Machine 503'd repeatedly. (b) Hedged citations survive nearly as often as confident ones (0.62 vs 0.69) — the models' own hedges were not tracking actual citation defects on this sample. (c) By source experiment: def-naming 0.68, backtest 0.61. (d) The 10 abstract-level non-confirmations are exactly that — claims not confirmable at abstract/title level within budget, NOT established misattributions; the three inspected closely look like over-specific attributions (a quote attributed to the Lenat & Marcus paper that appears in the companion obituary; "BDA popularized the burn-in term" unsupported at the primary; a "definition typology" claim invisible at abstract level of a 529-page treatise).

**Cost (measured).** 92 works: 99 search queries + 185 fetches total (mean 1.1 + 2.0 per work); ~1.06M subagent tokens for verification + ~0.54M for extraction; wall-clock ~16 min for the fan-out (8 parallel agents).

**Interpretation vs the prior anecdotes (bounded per review F5).** Within this sample, this task (owner-candidate proposal), and these models (opus/sonnet/GPT-5.x-era runs): **no confirmed fabrication among the cases successfully checked**, zero major bibliographic mismatches, and the residual risk concentrating in attribution precision and verification access. This does NOT overturn the prior anecdotes (5 fabrications, 2-for-2 defective citations on audited kills) — those came from a different population (load-bearing kill decisions, partly smaller models, full-text-level checks); 24 sampled occurrences remain existence-unresolved here, and abstract-level checking is shallower than the kills' primary-text audits. The C4 contract keeps its point either way; on this record its binding constraint was reachability.
