# Stripped the demos and the planner tanked

Been doing example strip runs on our support-ticket agent all week — pulled every worked example out of the prompt but left the step outline and formatting hints exactly where they were. Success rate went from 74% to 39%. That's a bigger drop than I expected given the outline was still telling it "first classify, then draft, then check tone." Apparently our model leans on seeing one solved ticket way more than it leans on being told the recipe in words. Worth noting example strip only touched the demonstrations, nothing else in the scaffold moved, so we can be fairly confident it's the demos carrying that weight and not some other prop we forgot to account for.

While I had the harness open I also tried a brain swap on the planning step, keeping the small model doing subtask execution but routing the plan generation through our cheapest tier. Score dropped from 74% to 58%, less catastrophic than the example strip hit but still real. Combined, this tells us the planner is doing more of the heavy lifting than the ticket-classification step itself, which nobody on the team believed until we saw both numbers side by side.

Also flagging: cold start on this same agent is brutal, first ticket in a fresh session averages 12 points below the session mean. We think it's because the agent hasn't yet inferred which of our three internal tools actually has the customer's order history. Anyone tuned their system prompt to shrink that ramp-up gap without just front-loading more examples?
