# Notes rot faster than you think

Been staring at claim survival tally numbers for our research-assistant agent all week and the trend is not good. On short tasks it's fine, high 80s, but past step 15 or so it falls off a cliff — down near 55% by the end of a 30-step run. The agent keeps writing things like "source confirms X" early on and then finds a contradicting source later without ever revisiting the earlier note. The final answer is usually still right because it weighs the last thing it read most heavily, but the trail behind it is full of dead claims.

We wanted to know if that was a robustness issue or just an artifact of task difficulty, so we ran the same tasks as pothole runs, injecting a search-API timeout partway through. Recovery time was fine, under two minutes on average, but the claim survival tally on the pothole runs was even worse than the clean baseline — the recovery process itself seems to generate a burst of new unverified notes that don't get reconciled.

Also worth flagging for the drift audit crowd: we included this claim survival tally metric in this month's frozen-suite comparison for the first time, and it dropped four points versus last month with no fault injection at all, same model version. Either the audit caught something real about a silent update, or our tally scorer has a bug. Digging into the scorer logs before we sound any alarms.
