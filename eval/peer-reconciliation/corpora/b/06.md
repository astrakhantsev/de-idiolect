# Robustness of Spreadsheet Verification

Agents verified a budget spreadsheet with cross-sheet formulas and supporting invoices. Intermediate assertion persistence ratio was estimated over all stated subtotal checks and provenance notes. The intermediate assertion persistence ratio was 0.77 for runs that maintained a single reconciliation table, compared with 0.46 for runs that restated totals in prose.

A seeded-defect audit placed one invoice with a duplicated line item into the input bundle midway through verification. In the seeded-defect audit, 34 of 50 agents isolated the duplicate before producing the final spreadsheet. Self-contradiction incidence was computed when final totals disagreed with a calculator or formula-inspection output from the same run. Self-contradiction incidence was 0.12 among containment passes and 0.37 among containment failures.

We randomized the order of otherwise equivalent invoice-search results across five repetitions. Permutation sensitivity collapse reduced correct duplicate detection from 76% to 49%, despite unchanged invoice content. The collapse was concentrated in agents that selected the first matching invoice as canonical without cross-checking the remaining results. The experiment therefore separates stable numerical verification from order-dependent document selection.
