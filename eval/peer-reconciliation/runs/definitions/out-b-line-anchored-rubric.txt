A judging procedure used when an answer, table, or completion output is scored against source or requested-field criteria. It examines the full transcript, not only the final response, and scores each decision for each answer, merged field, or record against specific transcript lines. Every credited criterion or scoring decision must cite the line containing its supporting evidence. It gives no credit to a claim, inference, classification, conflict resolution, or selected identity unless a cited line shows the retrieved evidence or explicitly states that the field is unresolved. Its result is an apparent accuracy score that is lower than unconstrained judging, measured by the percentage-point or percentage drop in accepted outputs after untraceable items are excluded.
