- Mechanism: ⟦TERM⟧ is computed/estimated over a specific set of stated intermediate claims (e.g., scratchpad claims, planning/extraction/draft notes, subtotal checks and provenance notes, or provisional conclusions) recorded during the task's execution trace, not over final outputs alone.
- Measurement: ⟦TERM⟧ is expressed as a numeric score (a value roughly between 0 and 1) derived from these retained claims/notes.
- Setting/trigger: it applies to multi-step agent task runs involving retrieval, extraction, or verification against external sources (repository queries, citations, invoices, policy excerpts) where intermediate claims are recorded before a final answer is produced.
- Constraint: the score is sensitive to whether early/provisional claims are later reused, restated, or reversed without being reconciled — e.g., copying provisional values into a final table, restating totals in prose instead of a single table, or reversing earlier conclusions lowers the score.
- Constraint: the score differs systematically between retrieval-first and inference-first strategies, with retrieval-first runs scoring higher.
- Constraint: the score is affected by the order in which supporting information arrives (e.g., excerpt order), not just its content.
