# Claims register — "The vocabulary seam" (FLF entry)

This file lists every load-bearing claim in the entry, one line per claim, with its evidence grade and support. It exists so a reader — human or model — can audit the entry's claims without re-deriving them from the prose. The entry is at https://github.com/astrakhantsev/de-idiolect/blob/main/ENTRY.md; the materials bundle (case study, pre-registrations, run receipts, review logs, novelty ledger) is linked from the entry's appendix; project-local terms are translated in the companion glossary (GLOSSARY.md), and protocol detail behind §5 is in the companion experiment log (EXPERIMENT-LOG.md).

**Grade legend** (the entry's palette, plus register-only labels): **measured** — a number on record from a run or a documented event · **measured (exploratory)** — real numbers from small or confounded runs · **measured (prior work)** — a published measurement by others, verified per the entry's citation note · **argued** — a mechanism or economic argument, no measurement · **hypothesis** — a stated expectation, untested · **proposal** — a design, not a capability claim · **unbuilt** — named but not implemented · **prior work** — an established result credited to others · **bounded null** — an absence claim limited to the searches on record. Pre-registered outcomes are marked **[pre-registered]**. Entry section references in brackets.

## Diagnosis

- **C01.** Cross-community knowledge fails to combine partly because search keys are private vocabulary: groups coin terms, search under them, and miss other groups' work on the same thing. — **measured (prior work)**: Blair & Maron 1985 (recall ~20% vs a stipulated 75% requirement); Larsen & Bong 2016 (9% average construct retrieval, 3% per-task low; 83% of participants would conclude a tested relationship "had not been tested"); Furnas et al. 1987 (probability two people choose the same term for the same thing: .07–.18). [§1]
- **C02.** The failure is invisible from inside: precision is observable, recall is not, so a clean search feels thorough even when it missed most of what exists. — **measured (prior work)**: Blair & Maron 1985 (observed precision ~80% alongside ~20% recall). [§1]
- **C03.** The failure's name is old: jangle fallacy = Kelley 1927 (pp. 63–64); jingle = Thorndike 1904 quoting Aikins. — **prior work** (attribution; primary-verified for Kelley). [§1]
- **C04.** Nothing in training rewards a model for volunteering "your term for this is not the field's term," so the failure improves in presentation while staying constant in substance; it did not self-correct in the tested case and cannot be assumed to self-correct with scale. — **argued**, with measured neighbours by analogy (Sharma et al. 2023: preference models reward convincing sycophancy; Perez et al. 2022: sycophancy grows with scale; CREPE 2023: false-presupposition checking fails largely via retrieval). [§1]
- **C05.** Capability helps recall through the neutral-definition channel, not around it: across eight model configurations (two families, four tiers), none recovered an opaque coinage's owner from the bare term, while a term-free definition of the same concept recovered the owner at the frontier models. — **measured**, n = 1 concept. [§1]
- **C06.** NOT claimed: that the terminology failure *worsens* with capability — the probe on that stronger claim came back "not shown." [§1]

## The documented case (the author's own project)

- **C07.** Across two consecutive research programs (~7,000 model calls, ~20 experimental legs in the second), the project produced roughly twenty vocabulary-level renamings — graded per row in the case-study ledger and not one class: ~3 genuine recall misses, ~10 knowing borrowings (the field's term cited at or near coinage), ~4 self-errors, ~4 rows still asserted or defective, the last excluded from any verified-renaming count. Three became headline novelty claims and were retracted: `m*` → Clemen & Winkler 1985; "operating requirement" → value-of-information threshold analysis (Raiffa & Schlaifer 1961 lineage, secondary); "the signal, not the cut" → Bröcker 2008, foreclosed for the exact theoretical claim under the theorem's conditions. Owners verified against primary text except where marked secondary. — **measured** (documented record). [§2]
- **C08.** Every retraction was preceded by a confident null: a novelty search in the project's own vocabulary that found nothing and looked thorough. — **measured** (on the record). [§2]
- **C09.** Knowing about the failure mode did not prevent it: the day-one scoping declared a terminology sweep owed, a pre-mortem named the risk, and a mid-program sweep concluded "novelty survives" one week before both Program-A claims were found predated. — **measured** (on the record). [§2]
- **C10.** Same-briefed agent agreement supplied no independent evidence: a 6-of-6 agent consensus was one search run six times; a refuter assigned a disjoint literature overturned one such consensus in minutes. — **measured** (one documented instance). [§2]
- **C11.** Countermeasure pilot, run on the project's own record after the retractions. Setup: for four of the project's coinages, an isolated model generated a term-free definition from era-gated excerpts (only documents predating each term's retraction, so definitions could not inherit the answers); blind models were then asked what the concept is called and who owns it — three draws given the definition (the definition arm) versus two draws given the raw coined term with its in-project gloss (the control arm), predictions sealed per case. Result: the definition arm recovered the hidden owner on the one *opaque* coinage, where the control was actively misrouted by the coinage's surface words. The advantage is union-level only: pooling candidates across each arm's draws, the definition arm reached the owner's family on 2 of 4 concepts versus the control's 1 of 4, while per-draw medians tied. Caveats: all draws came from one model family; the era gate is retraction-dated, not discovery-dated (closed by §5.3's pre-discovery freeze); and the comparison varies information content, not vocabulary alone — discovery evidence, not an isolation of neutrality. — **measured (exploratory)**, n = 4, one clean comparative cell. [§2]
- **C12.** Discovery datum from one of the pilot's confounded cells (the coinage "read the enumerations, not the votes," whose cell supports no clean arm comparison): two definition-arm draws independently named Ho, Hull & Srihari 1994 as the true owner of the concept's combination mechanics — an owner the project's three earlier prior-art passes (model-run searches, human-audited) had missed, and one the project's own answer key (the internal glossary row recording that coinage's believed owner) had mis-mapped. Verified against the primary text. — **measured** (candidate-discovery evidence, not clean comparison). [§2]
- **C13.** The value regime (transparency gradient): de-idiolection earns its place on opaque coinages over non-obvious owners; transparent slogans carry themselves; already-known fields need citation discipline, not definitions. — **hypothesis** from the 4-case pilot (per-case predictions sealed; the cross-case pattern read after the fact). [§2]

## History and economics

- **C14.** Universal + manual + build-once shared-vocabulary resources failed repeatedly, for recurring causes named in their own post-mortems: acquisition cost, maintenance cost, incentives, coordination, funding collapse. — **argued** (a synthesis over selected critiques: Hoekstra 2010, Doctorow 2001, Shirky 2003, ALPAC 1966; "every failed project" is not claimable — no full case matrix exists). [§3]
- **C15.** What changed: LLM generation makes the marginal cost of one more definition cheap — addressing acquisition directly and maintenance only insofar as regeneration substitutes for re-curation; incentives and coordination are addressed by the architecture, not by cost; the system's own lifecycle costs (detection, verification, provenance, disputes) are not priced. — **argued** (unmeasured economics). [§3]

## The proposed workflow

- **C16.** The pipeline: detect community-local term use → generate a short operational definition in a small fixed vocabulary of ordinary words (no names of people, methods, or fields) → use the definition, not the term, as the retrieval key, with SKOS-typed relations, pairwise verification, and abstention as a first-class outcome. Matching runs over supplied corpora; discovering which communities to search is unbuilt. — **proposal**. [§4.1]
- **C17.** Whether ordinary words strip *enough* community identity for cross-community neutrality — **hypothesis**; the only tests (eggs run + ablation) isolate specificity, not neutrality, and the ablation was inconclusive. [§4.1, §5.1, appendix]
- **C18.** Key/representation split: retrieval keys may be cheap, lossy, and plural; the faithful representation is required only at verification time, behind a fidelity gate. Fidelity-before-retrieval remains the default pipeline ordering, and no candidate mapping proceeds to verification or publication without a gate-passing representation. — **proposal**, motivated by measured results (drifted definitions still routed, §5.3; fusion null, §5.4). [§4.1]
- **C19.** Generator-as-component behind a contract (round-trip entailment + external-name ban + lexical constraint): any implementation whose output passes satisfies the architecture. One measured step: checklist-guided regeneration cleared the fidelity half 2/2 where unguided failed 0/2; no implementation, including this prototype's, has demonstrated full compliance; wordlist enforcement unbuilt. — **proposal** with one measured leg. [§4.1, §5.4]
- **C20.** Design commitments: no universal resource is ever built; the shared layer, when present, is a derived, regenerable, versioned, forkable cache — never a canonical merge; disagreement is a typed link. Compounding-without-agreement is the intended property. — **proposal / hypothesis** (commons unbuilt); prior owners of the stance credited: Star & Griesemer 1989, Bowker & Star 1999, emergent semantics, dataspaces, Relink. [§4.1]
- **C21.** The one shared artifact — a fixed generic defining wordlist — is excluded from the graveyard economics because such wordlists need very little curation (LDOCE precedent: revised a few times in decades). — **argued**. [§4.1]
- **C22.** The project's verified failures sort into three classes mapping onto the pipeline's stages: missed coining-moments (detection, ~3), definition/estimand drift (definition, ~4), defective provenance (verification: both checked retractions had real quotes with defective citations; ~85 review findings included zero erroneous computed values). — **measured** taxonomy (small counts, stated). [§4.2]
- **C23.** Three inward interventions — coin-time interception, claim-time estimand self-audit, cite-time provenance check — are **proposals** grounded in that taxonomy, not tested capabilities; the retrospective version of coin-time interception was effectively already run and was near-null on fresh cases (scope = C13's gradient). [§4.2]
- **C24.** Cite-time provenance yield on the project's own citations: in a seeded sample of 100 model-proposed citation occurrences, 66/100 survived at the lenient primary endpoint; 43/91 at the strictest sensitivity (nine ineligible dropped); zero confirmed fabrications and zero major bibliographic mismatches among checkable cases; attrition dominated by publisher access walls and attribution precision; cost ~1 search + 2 fetches per work. — **measured** (amended endpoint, abstract-level depth). [§4.2]
- **C25.** Rescue framing: the project was not rescued because it queried late, in its own vocabulary, with same-model fan-out — not because the knowledge was unreachable (the programs also failed on separate technical grounds, §7); for rescue, model memorization is an asset, and the tool's job is firing the lookup at the right time in the right vocabulary. NOT claimed: that the tool finds things a frontier model does not know. — **argued**. [§4.2]
- **C26.** Hook vs. standing instruction: the agents that ran the failed "novelty survives" sweep operated under standing instructions to search the field's vocabulary first — installed, and the failure happened anyway; a deterministic hook fires regardless of attention. The prompt half is measured on this record; the hook half is **argued and unbuilt**. The architecture beyond the hook adds verification, blindness, grounding, and the commons — each answering a measured failure. [§4.2]

## Experiments

- **C27.** Eggs routing check: for three oracle-supplied specialist concepts, the term-free constrained definition pulls the owning sub-field's best document to rank 1 where the naive lay question buries it at 4–8 (bge-large-en-v1.5, 15 documents, offline, deterministic; live-generated definitions reproduced the routing). The isolated effect is **specificity, not vocabulary-neutrality** (the ablation varied that axis and found no rank difference; on the one metric with headroom the free-text arm was ahead). — **measured**, n = 3 concepts, one case, one model. [§5.1, appendix]
- **C28.** Curated-vocabulary negative [pre-registered]: the first cell triggered its success rule at a threshold later shown to sit at chance (claims layered on it retracted under three reviews); a systematic second-cell search found no valid test pair because curated synonyms are memorized by construction — any pair prominent enough for a curated thesaurus is already reconciled in training corpora (measured on the vocabularies, models, and cutoffs searched; generalization is an argument). Clean regimes narrow to post-cutoff concepts and private coinages. — **measured**. [§5.2]
- **C29.** End-to-end cell negative [pre-registered]: both answer-blind constrained definitions failed the frozen round-trip fidelity gate on the same feature (prospectivity) before retrieval could be scored — definitional drift replicated 2/2 on fresh generations; fidelity, not leakage, is where generation first failed its contract. Separately (outside the pre-registration), the retrospective detection demo missed its endpoint (coinage ranked ~224). A grounded direct-model baseline with the same excerpts and corpus put the true owner literature at ranks 1–2, so the pipeline's mechanical form earned no measured margin on this cell. — **measured**. [§5.3]
- **C30.** Gate calibration [pre-registered]: four faithful definitions all pass (false positives 0/4); five seeded single-defect definitions all fail on the planted item (detection 5/5, attribution 5/5). Bounded: author-constructed materials, four defect axes — a non-degeneracy check, not a population false-positive estimate. — **measured**. [§5.4]
- **C31.** Checklist-guided fix [pre-registered]: a leak-clean checklist extracted from the frozen excerpts guided regeneration that passes the gate 2/2 vs 0/2 unguided, including the item both original configs failed. n = 1 per config, same machinery, no contemporaneous unguided replicate; a stricter prospectivity reading is arguably still unmet. — **measured** (consistency, not causality). [§5.4]
- **C32.** Fidelity bought no routing: the two gate-passing definitions rank the first owner 2 and 3 — no better than the drifted definitions they replace; fidelity is a validity requirement, not a performance lever. — **measured (exploratory)**. [§5.4]
- **C33.** Union-of-keys null [pre-registered]: under a frozen reciprocal-rank-fusion rule, no union of keys weakly dominated the best single key (the bare coinage), and every fusion promoted the corpus's misroute document. The multi-key proposal stays conjectural with a one-cell null attached. — **measured**. [§5.4]
- **C34.** Sealed peer-reconciliation test [pre-registered]: on synthetic two-community corpora (10 planted pairs per key; no live-community claim), the sealed held-out key missed the pre-registered bar — 5/10 correct at every pre-registered operating point (required ≥ 7). Transferable operating characteristics: detection precision 1.00 at every pre-registered operating point, measured on the later keys and all sampling draws — the only two false positives in eight scored runs occurred in one early iteration and never recurred after their prompt-level cause was reverted; all five hard relation assertions typed correctly; coverage 0.6 (three pairs exhausted generation-stage artifact gates, one failed quote validation); iterating against one key overfit exactly as the protocol assumed (dev-counterfactual 8/10 → fresh-key 4/10). — **measured** (sealed held-out; synthetic). [§5.5]

## Novelty position

- **C35.** The pipeline's *stages and several sub-assemblies* are prior work, credited by name: the spine — generate definitions → embed → match → type relations (SciCo-Radar: generated definitions + typed relations; GenOM: definition-embedding as retrieval key + pairwise verification; EDC, structurally closest: extract → define → canonicalize-by-definition-vector; KGGen; pre-LLM ancestor CID, Larsen & Bong 2016); detection→definition as a sub-assembly (Microsoft patent family, NeoN, AXOLOTL'24); the lazy/no-global-resource stance for single-community use (emergent semantics, dataspaces, Confluence "Define" (secondary)). — **prior work**, verified per the citation note's surfaces; per-item detail in the dossiers. [§6]
- **C36.** The *full assembly as one system* — detection-triggered, constrained-vocabulary definitions used as cross-community matching keys with SKOS-typed relations and no canonical merge — was NOT found: two blind passes reproduced the prior-art map and found no fused system, and the nearest full-assembly neighbours (LAVOHA, VSS) are contrasts that build merged canonical resources. This is deliberately graded "not found in the searches on record" — a bounded null, not a novelty claim: the searches are one model family plus one web surface, the ledger is draft, and it kept finding closer neighbours (EDC surfaced only in the second blind pass). Same status for the narrower strip: a controlled defining vocabulary for machine-generated cross-community definitions (weakened by the inconclusive ablation). The entry does not depend on novelty. — **bounded null**. [§6]

## Scaling and compounding

- **C37.** Value concentrates on the coined / post-cutoff / genuinely-parallel frontier: memorized (reconciled) seams make the tool redundant, and grounded inference over both communities' texts is the same operation the pipeline performs, not a shortcut around it; the claim is inherently marginal over asking a frontier model directly with the same corpus access — a baseline that rises every generation. Bounded from both sides by C28 (curated pairs memorized) and C29 (equal-information baseline matched the pipeline). — **argued**, with measured corroborations. [§7]
- **C38.** Compounding without agreement (the commons) is the design target and a **design property, not a demonstrated outcome** — the prototype does not run the commons. — **hypothesis**. [§7]

## Implementation status

- **C39.** The build inventory, as one row (status inventory, not a graded claim; source: §7 and the appendix component-status table). *Built and runnable*: candidate-term surfacing (runs; missed its retrospective demo endpoint, §5.3); embedding retrieval/matching (§5.1's measurement, offline, deterministic); definition generation live behind an operational interface (routing held; full contract compliance, including the enforced wordlist, not demonstrated); relation typing scaffolded in the §5.1 prototype with no live run there — live-measured only on the separate synthetic §5.5 harness, with real-community deployment unrun. *Run once, exploratory*: the naming pilot (§2), the vocabulary ablation (appendix), the end-to-end cell (§5.3), the follow-up measurement set (§5.4). *Not built*: a live-generation superiority evaluation on a real corpus, scaling beyond n = 3 / one case / one model, precision–recall with relevance judgments, the inward workflow as tooling, the commons. [§7, appendix]
